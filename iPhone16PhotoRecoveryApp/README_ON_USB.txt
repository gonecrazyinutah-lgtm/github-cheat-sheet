HOW TO USE:
-----------
- Windows/Mac/TV: Plug USB, open folders. If HEIC doesn't show, use iPhone16_Photos but convert HEIC->JPG, or use Organized_By_Date.
- iPhone: Files app > USB drive > tap photo to view
- To prove nothing was altered: Compare current file hashes to verification_hashes.txt
  On Mac: shasum -a 256 file.jpg and compare to list
  On Windows: certutil -hashfile file.jpg SHA256
